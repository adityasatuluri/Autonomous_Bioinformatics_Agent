MCP Route Guide & Prompt Library

Explore 5 specialized agent routes, question types, algorithms, and 15 interactive templates

Route 1 • Drug Interaction MCP Targeted Therapies
🎯 Question Scope (Q/A Intent):

Targetable kinase receptors, small molecule inhibitors, FDA-approved antineoplastics, compound repurposing, and pharmacogenomic targeting for differential biomarkers.

⚙️ What the Agent Does:

Cross-references top upregulated genes against DGIdb and ChEMBL databases, evaluates drug-gene binding mechanisms, and scores actionable therapeutic targets.

Hypothesis Templates (Click to apply):
Which top overexpressed genes in Breast cancer represent targetable drug receptors or have existing FDA-approved inhibitors?
Identify candidate therapeutic targets and small molecule drug interactions for upregulated genes in Colorectal tumors.
Are there any repurposable antineoplastic drugs or kinase inhibitors targeting the leading biomarkers in Glioblastoma?
Route 2 • STRING Network MCP Interactome Topology
🎯 Question Scope (Q/A Intent):

Protein-protein interaction (PPI) networks, interactome degree centrality, hub driver genes, master regulator bottleneck proteins, and module connectivity.

⚙️ What the Agent Does:

Queries STRING-DB v12 REST API, constructs a confidence-scored interactome graph (score ≥ 0.7), and computes node degree rankings to isolate master regulatory hubs.

Hypothesis Templates (Click to apply):
What are the key hub driver genes and protein-protein interaction network topology connecting the top biomarkers?
Map the protein interactome and identify central hub nodes with highest degree centrality among significant genes.
Construct a STRING PPI network to reveal core functional modules and master regulator bottleneck proteins in Lung cancer.
Route 3 • GO Secretion MCP Liquid Biopsy
🎯 Question Scope (Q/A Intent):

Liquid biopsy feasibility, non-invasive biomarker detection, extracellular space shed proteins, exosome vesicle cargo, and plasma diagnostic profiling.

⚙️ What the Agent Does:

Queries Gene Ontology QuickGO API for cellular components (GO:0005576 extracellular space, GO:0070062 exosome) and scores secretome suitability for blood-based diagnostic assays.

Hypothesis Templates (Click to apply):
Which candidate biomarkers are secreted proteins or shed into extracellular vesicles suitable for liquid biopsy blood tests?
Evaluate the cellular secretion and extracellular space localization of top differentially expressed genes for non-invasive diagnostics.
Are the leading cancer-specific platelet markers detectable in blood plasma or circulating microvesicles?
Route 4 • Clinical Survival MCP Prognosis & Hazard
🎯 Question Scope (Q/A Intent):

Prognostic biomarker validation, overall survival (OS) outcomes, recurrence-free survival, Kaplan-Meier curves, and patient risk stratification.

⚙️ What the Agent Does:

Queries TCGA Pan-Cancer Clinical Atlas cohorts, computes log-rank Kaplan-Meier p-values and Cox proportional hazard ratios, stratifying patients into high vs low risk.

Hypothesis Templates (Click to apply):
Do these differentially expressed genes correlate with overall survival outcomes and patient prognosis in TCGA cohorts?
Assess the prognostic hazard ratio and survival risk associated with elevated expression of candidate genes.
Which identified gene markers stratify high-risk versus low-risk clinical cohorts and predict recurrence-free survival?
Route 5 • Reactome & PubMed MCP Signaling & Novelty
🎯 Question Scope (Q/A Intent):

Biological pathway cascades, signaling perturbations, statistical overrepresentation (FDR), literature evidence cross-checking, and candidate biomarker novelty.

⚙️ What the Agent Does:

Performs pathway enrichment via Reactome Analysis Service, queries PubMed and Europe PMC APIs for disease co-citations, and categorizes genes into confirmed vs novel markers.

Hypothesis Templates (Click to apply):
What biological pathways are significantly perturbed and which top genes represent novel uncharacterized biomarkers?
Perform Reactome signaling pathway enrichment and cross-check PubMed literature for documented disease associations.
Investigate pathway cascades and verify whether our top candidate biomarkers have prior published literature evidence.
